# Dr. Mia — Product Positioning

**A calmer front desk. Better-prepared appointments. More connected patients.**

Dr. Mia is a character-led dental-practice assistant designed to help clinics handle patient inquiries, collect pre-visit history, coordinate appointment communications, and learn from patient feedback. Clinic staff remain in control of important decisions and handoffs.

## Product modules

### AI Front Desk — planned
Capture after-hours and overflow inquiries, including a future voice/phone integration. Collect the caller's reason for contact, callback details, and appointment preferences, then route a clear summary to staff. An appointment request must not be described as confirmed unless a trusted scheduling workflow confirms it.

### Smart Patient Intake — prototype
Let patients complete a clinic-approved history before the visit. Organize reported symptoms, dental history, relevant conditions, current medicines, allergies, and reactions into a readable dentist-facing brief. The current demo uses synthetic data, and the questionnaire remains a draft awaiting qualified review.

### Appointment messaging — planned
Send confirmations from verified booking events, reminders (including a one-hour reminder where configured), and appropriate follow-up. The current prototype does not send messages or book appointments.

### Patient feedback — planned
Collect optional feedback about waiting time, communication, comfort, and the overall experience. Summarize themes for clinic improvement and invite honest public reviews without filtering requests based on private feedback scores.

### Optional Growth / Lead Engine — proposed
Capture inquiries from clinic-approved campaigns, track their source, route callbacks, and report outcomes when a confirmed appointment can be verified. Marketing follow-up requires appropriate permission and opt-out. Clinical records must not be repurposed as marketing lists.

### Clinician-facing medication support — future concept
A later, separately evaluated module may surface traceable evidence and patient-specific safety considerations for a dentist's review. It is not implemented today and must not independently diagnose, prescribe, or transmit medication orders.

## Suggested homepage copy

**Headline:** Meet Dr. Mia, your dental clinic's AI co-pilot.

**Subheadline:** Capture after-hours inquiries, prepare patient histories before appointments, keep patients informed, and turn feedback into better clinic experiences, with your team in control.

**CTA:** Book a 10-minute front-desk workflow review.

## Honest launch boundary

The repository currently contains a reference WhatsApp workflow, testable inquiry logic, and a local clinical demo using synthetic data. Live phone answering, production messaging, appointment scheduling, patient review campaigns, and medication recommendations are not shipped capabilities. Measure time savings and business outcomes in a real pilot before making claims about them.
