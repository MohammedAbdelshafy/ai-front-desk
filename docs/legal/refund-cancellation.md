# Refund & Cancellation Policy — Mia Dental OS

> **DRAFT v0.1 — 2026-10-11 — requires qualified legal review before production use or real patient data.**
> This is a starting draft, not legal advice. It has not been reviewed by an attorney.

**Operator:** MBM (Mohammed Abdelshafy), contact: abdelshafyclapps@gmail.com

## 1. Free trial

The 7-day free trial is **free**. No payment method is collected and no charges are made during the trial. There is nothing to refund for the trial period.

## 2. Cancellation

- You may cancel your subscription **at any time** from your account page — no phone call, no retention flow.
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period. You keep full access until then.
- After cancellation, your account moves to expired-trial status: paid features lock, but your data is preserved under our [Data Retention & Deletion](data-retention-deletion.md) policy.

## 3. Refunds (paid plan)

- **Billing is currently in test mode** — no real charges are processed while we onboard pilot clinics, so no refunds are applicable yet.
- Once live billing begins: because the trial gives you 7 days of full access before any charge, **subscription fees are generally non-refundable** once a billing period has started.
- Exceptions we will honor: duplicate charges, charges made after a timely cancellation, or charges resulting from our billing error — refunded in full, no questions asked. Email abdelshafyclapps@gmail.com with your account email and invoice reference.
- Refund requests for other reasons are reviewed case-by-case within 30 days of the charge.

## 4. What happens to your data on cancellation

See [Data Retention & Deletion](data-retention-deletion.md). Your records are retained for a defined window after cancellation so you can export them or resubscribe; then deleted on request or per the schedule. Anonymized audit minimums may be retained as described there.

## 5. Price changes

If the subscription price changes, we will notify you at least 30 days before the change applies to your renewal, and you may cancel before the new price takes effect.
