## C. Staff privacy and acceptable-use notice

- Staff access is limited to approved job duties and clinic assignments.
- Do not export records, copy data to personal devices, paste identifiable clinical data into unapproved AI assistants, or use patient data for model training.
- Record viewing, changes, export, deletion, evidence approval and break-glass access according to the approved audit policy.
- Staff must report suspected misdelivery, patient misbinding, lost device, credential exposure, unsafe clinical output or unauthorized access immediately through [incident contact/process].
- Training owner: [name]; training frequency and evidence: [approve].
