# Mia Dental OS — Clinical Reference Review Checklist

**Dataset under review:** reference v2 (2026-10-11)
**Packet:** `REFERENCE_REVIEW_PACK.md` (generated from the app's reference code — what you read is what the app uses)

## What we are asking you to do (plain language)

Review the **accuracy of the reference content** — drug entries, dosing, allergy
alternatives, cautions, safety rules, and citations — against current, authoritative
guidance (FDA-approved labeling, ADA/AHA statements, WHO guidance, or your national
equivalent).

**You are reviewing reference accuracy. You are NOT:**
- endorsing the Mia Dental OS software,
- validating clinical outcomes or the app's safety performance,
- granting any regulatory clearance,
- being asked to approve the app for patient use (that requires separate steps).

If you find anything wrong, outdated, or missing, flag it — that is the most
valuable outcome of this review.

## How to review

1. Read each drug entry in Section 1 of the review pack.
2. For each entry, check against the cited source (URL included) and your clinical
   knowledge:
   - [ ] Drug name, aliases, and class are correct
   - [ ] Standard adult dosing is current and appropriate for dental use
   - [ ] Maximum daily dose is correct
   - [ ] Notes/cautions are accurate and complete
   - [ ] Cited sources actually support the entry
3. Review each indication panel (Section 2): first-line choices, allergy
   alternatives, and cautions.
4. Review each safety rule (Section 3): trigger, severity (RED/YELLOW), and reason.
5. Review counseling flags (Section 4).
6. Answer the general questions below.
7. Complete the sign-off blocks. For any item you cannot approve, write the
   issue in "Findings" instead of signing that section.
